- **Consultant / Senior Software Engineer, targens (LBBW)** <span style="float:right">2018 – 2021</span><br>Built DLT-based financial products bridging distributed ledger networks with banking systems (SEPA, Swift, TARGET2). Developed data anonymization tools for GDPR compliance on IBM Mainframe infrastructure.

- **Secretary / Director of Technology, Jan Dhan Co-operative Credit Society** <span style="float:right">2017 – 2018</span><br>Co-founded a microfinance institution. Pioneered branchless digital banking using India Stack, biometrics, and digital payment channels.

- **Technology Consultant, SUMO Instruments** <span style="float:right">2015 – 2016</span><br>Led IoT software architecture for Smart Fuel Tankers, including cloud-based monitoring and OTA remote control.

- **Co-Founder, Zamosa** <span style="float:right">2013 – 2014</span><br>Co-developed a location-based mobile marketing platform for offline retail in Europe, integrating beacon technology with data-driven recommender systems.

- **Co-Founder, Voicedrop** <span style="float:right">2012 – 2013</span><br>Co-developed a patent-pending media format using WebRTC for interactive teaching and learning. Secured angel investment.
