Physicist by training, currently working on machine intelligence and quantum computing. Diplom Physik and DFG-funded doctoral research in stellar astrophysics at Göttingen. Earlier, Q-learning and Lagrangian-mechanics work on artificial muscle systems at the Max Planck Institute for Dynamics and Self-Organization — early reinforcement-learning research, predating the deep-RL era and the rise of physics-informed machine learning. Today, building QMI Lab — independent research on quantum machine learning and world models — and AstroLLM, a domain-specialized language model for astronomy.
