--- layout: default title: Nandan Joshi — physicist working on machine intelligence and quantum computing ---

Physicist by training, currently working on machine intelligence and quantum computing. Diplom Physik and DFG-funded doctoral research in stellar astrophysics at Göttingen. Earlier, Q-learning and Lagrangian-mechanics work on artificial-muscle systems at the Max Planck Institute for Dynamics and Self-Organization — early reinforcement-learning research, predating the deep-RL era and the rise of physics-informed machine learning. Active research at QMI Lab — quantum machine learning and world models — and AstroLLM, an open-source domain-specialized language model for astronomy.
