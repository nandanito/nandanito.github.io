## Earlier Research

**DFG Doctoral Research Fellow — Institut für Astrophysik, Göttingen** <span class="cv-meta">Göttingen · Jun 2007 – May 2012</span>

Doctoral research on a Deutsche Forschungsgemeinschaft fellowship. Studied stellar rotation and magnetic activity in late-type stars — specifically M-dwarfs, the primary hosts of habitable-zone exoplanet candidates.

Selected work:

- **Observational astrophysics** — multiple campaigns at European Southern Observatory facilities in Chile, collecting high-resolution spectroscopic data on target stars.
- **Spectroscopic data analysis pipelines** — custom IDL tools for spectrum reduction, activity indicators, and rotational broadening measurements.
- **Scientific communication** — public lectures and a newspaper article on astrophysics for general audiences.

Output: peer-reviewed paper in *The Astronomical Journal* (Reiners et al. 2012, see Publications).

**Research and Teaching Assistant — Department of Physics, Göttingen** <span class="cv-meta">Göttingen · Dec 2004 – May 2007</span>

Pre-doctoral period at the Department of Physics, spanning research at the Institute for X-Ray Physics and undergraduate teaching. X-ray scattering work using analytical (Ray Theory) and numerical (Mathematica) approaches with applications in material sciences and biophysics; carried into the Diplom thesis on X-ray absorption and scattering by small particles via classical Mie Theory.

**Scientific Assistant — Institut für Zoologie und Anthropologie, Göttingen** <span class="cv-meta">Göttingen · Jul 2003 – Jan 2004</span>

DFG-funded research project — animalbase.org — an open scientific resource for historical zoological literature and species descriptions. Designed the underlying database and built a custom expert-crowdsourcing web platform (Apache Tomcat, JSP, Oracle RDBMS) for distributed taxonomic contribution. Co-developed the integration with the Göttingen State and University Library's ancient-literature digitization project — bridging historical zoological texts and structured taxonomic data, in what would now be called scientific knowledge graph work.

**Research Assistant — Max Planck Institute for Dynamics and Self-Organization** <span class="cv-meta">Göttingen · Nov 2002 – Jul 2003</span>

Nonlinear dynamics group.

Selected work:

- **Reinforcement learning for artificial-muscle systems** — developed Q-learning agents controlling simulated artificial-muscle architectures, with the underlying biomechanical dynamics modeled through Lagrangian mechanics. Reinforcement learning combined with classical mechanics, in 2002 — more than a decade before deep RL and close to two decades before physics-informed ML emerged as named research areas.
- **Pattern recognition** — collaborated on pattern-matching work inspired by the feline visual cortex, contributing to the institute's broader research on pattern formation in biological and computational systems.

## Publications

**Reiners, A., Joshi, N., Goldman, B.** *A Catalog of Rotation and Activity in Early-M Stars.* The Astronomical Journal, **143**, 93 (2012). [ADS](http://adsabs.harvard.edu/abs/2012AJ....143...93R)

Catalog of 334 M dwarfs with high-resolution optical spectra for 206 targets, projected rotational velocities (vsin i), and Hα emission measurements. Statistical study of the rotation–activity relationship across the boundary to full convection.
